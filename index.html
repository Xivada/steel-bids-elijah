Why steel

Built for how Houston builds

Straight every time

Cold-formed steel (CFS) studs come out to plan. No crown, warp or twist.

Factory holes let plumbing, electrical and HVAC run through without drilling.

Won't rot

Steel doesn't feed mold or rot in Houston's humidity.

Questions

Straight answers

Does steel cost more than wood?

As material, sometimes. That's why we price your actual plan. Compare the whole picture: package, labor, carry and callbacks.

Will my framers know how to use it?

Steel is screwed together, not nailed. Ask about installation and training when we review your bid.

What about thermal bridging?

Steel conducts heat, so walls need a thermal break. Most builders use continuous insulation (CI). We'll get you the engineering answer for your plan.

What's the lead time?

It depends on the job. We'll give you the real number with your bid.

What does the bid cost?
